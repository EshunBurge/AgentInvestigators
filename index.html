About the academy

How training works

Every lesson follows the same five steps: read the briefing, pass a vocabulary test, learn the big idea, answer a short quiz, then prove it in a field simulation. Finish a chapter and you earn a certificate.

1

Create your agent

Sign up, pick the game version or the regular version, and build an agent with your own look.

2

Take the lesson

Read the first half, pass the vocabulary test, then learn the concepts and answer a quick quiz.

3

Run the simulation

Each lesson ends with a hands-on test: sort evidence, spot AI mistakes, or work with a practice AI.

4

Level up

Earn coins, build a daily streak, collect badges, and spend coins on gear for your agent.

Good to know

Students in grades 9 to 12, ages 13 to 18. Teachers and parents are welcome to follow along.

The game version adds levels, coins, gear and Field Ops missions. The regular version keeps the same lessons, tests and quizzes with a simple checklist layout. You can switch any time in your profile.

Plan on 20 to 40 minutes. You can stop between steps and pick up where you left off.

You can continue as a guest and save progress on your device, but an account saves it to the cloud so you can play anywhere.

The practice tools are simulations that behave like an AI helper so you can learn safely. Never share real private information, even in practice.
